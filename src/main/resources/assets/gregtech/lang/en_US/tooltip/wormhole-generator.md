Transfers {EU} between two wormhole generators
Wormholes are linked by placing an AE2 Entangled Singularity in each controller slot
The transfer rate is limited by the wormhole size, and the wormhole size is governed by the transfer rate
If the transfer rate is completely stable, the transfer efficiency is {color_efficiency:{var:efficiency}%}
{EU} will only be transferred if there is space in the laser source hatch
Each laser target hatch must have a laser source hatch on the {italic:other} controller, on the {italic:opposite} side
Consumes an AE2 Singularity from an input bus each time the wormhole is kick-started
Right click the controller with a screwdriver to disable overclocking
