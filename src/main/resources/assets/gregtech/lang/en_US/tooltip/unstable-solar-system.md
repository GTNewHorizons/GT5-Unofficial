A stabilized star suspended in a bounded spacetime pocket. Where a classic Eye of
Harmony mines planets, this system hosts a living star: insert a {gold}USS Controller{gray}
into the controller slot and the star ignites at a tier set by the
{blue}Spacetime Compression Field Generator{gray} tier (tiers 0-2 main-sequence,
3-5 white dwarf, 6-8 supermassive).
{gold:{hr:87}}
The star has a finite lifespan (shown with a scanner) and burns out when it is
spent — consuming the controller in the process. One controller, one star life.
A new star can be lit at any time by inserting a fresh controller.
{gold:{hr:87}}
The system has no recipe of its own: its purpose is to anchor the ships and
structures that will one day circle it.
{gold:{hr:87}}

of Harmony; both can exist in the same world.
